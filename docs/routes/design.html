Architecture

Design Specifications

Engineered to stay out of your way and deliver zero-flicker UI interactions.

1. Layout Shift Prevention

Uses useLayoutEffect to perform all DOM measurements and scroll coordinate adjustments synchronously before browser screen paint.

2. Debounced Storage Operations

Scroll position writes to sessionStorage are debounced at 100ms to eliminate main thread blocking during active user scrolling.

3. O(1) ResizeObserver

useAvailableHeight observes element and parent layout shifts with ResizeObserver and requestAnimationFrame without thread polling or loops.
